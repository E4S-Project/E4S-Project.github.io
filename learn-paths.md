Learning Paths are concrete sequences through E4S Learning and the rest of the site, organized by role.

## Newcomers and Explorers

Graduate students, postdocs, and new staff getting oriented.

1. [Orientation](/learn-orientation/)
2. [Quick Starts](/learn-quick-starts/) — start with the general [E4S Quick Start](/quick-start/)
3. [Core Competencies](/learn-core/)
4. [E4S Product Families](/about-product-families/) — see what's available

## Application Developers

Domain scientists and research programmers building or modernizing applications.

1. [Orientation](/learn-orientation/)
2. [Quick Starts](/learn-quick-starts/) — pick the one matching your stack (PyTorch, TensorFlow, NeMo, BioNeMo, vLLM, HYPRE, LAMMPS, or ParaView)
3. [Core Competencies](/learn-core/) — especially [composing multiple libraries](/kokkos-hypre/)
4. [Advanced Topics](/learn-advanced/) — the product-family guide matching your domain

## Software and Infrastructure Engineers

Those responsible for build systems, packaging, CI/CD, containers, and deployment.

1. [Get E4S](/get/) — installation methods: Spack builds, build caches, containers
2. [Core Competencies](/learn-core/) — package and build fundamentals
3. [Advanced Topics](/learn-advanced/) — facility-scale deployment ([Frontier](/frontier-container-demo/), [Frank](/frank-cluster-instructions/)) and [testing your own tools against E4S](/test-with-e4s/)
4. [Use E4S](/use/) — [Documentation](/documentation/) and the [Guide Bot](/guide-e4s-bot/) for ongoing reference

## Facility, Program, and Project Stakeholders

Center leadership, program managers, and vendors.

1. [Introduction to E4S](/about-e4s-intro/) — mission and stewardship framing
2. [E4S Product Families](/about-product-families/)
3. [Get E4S](/get/) — overview of deployment options
4. [Join E4S](/join/) — for organizations wanting to participate
